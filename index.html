About

Radio is part technical hobby, part community, and part adventure.

Amateur radio brings together electronics, antennas, computers, emergency communications, public service, experimentation, and conversations with people around the world. Whether operating from home, a vehicle, a park, or a temporary field station, there is always something new to learn.

The focus here is practical: reliable equipment, clean installations, safe mobile operation, useful accessories, antenna experimentation, and making the station easier and more enjoyable to use.
