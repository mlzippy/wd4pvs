Operating

Built to communicate and experiment.

The station focuses on practical, reliable operating with room to test new antennas, accessories, software, and radio techniques.

Operating interests

  • VHF/UHF FM simplex and repeaters
  • Mobile and road-trip operating
  • HF voice and digital experimentation
  • Local nets, clubs, and public-service activity
  • Antenna and feed-line testing
  • Technical projects and station accessories
